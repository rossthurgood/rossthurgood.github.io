Project review

 

Overall, the feedback was largely positive, with reviewers praising the project’s visual quality and immersive atmosphere. They highlighted the dark colour palette and cinematic camera movement as particularly effective, and many liked that each planet was procedurally generated because it made every visit feel unique. The scroll-driven storytelling was also described as engaging and easy to follow. However, some users noted that certain text stayed on screen for too long and that performance could be improved on lower-powered devices. A few also felt that some sections could benefit from a slightly faster pace. Overall, reviewers agreed that the project successfully combined storytelling with interactive 3D web technology and would make a strong portfolio piece.
